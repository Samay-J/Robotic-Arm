The numbers follow the sliders. The arm only moves when you press Move arm.

Type where the gripper tip should be. IK works out the joint angles.

Pitch 0 = gripper pointing forward, negative = pointing down. If the target is solvable, the sliders jump to the answer so you can compare them with the FK numbers above.

Output appears here.

Edit the numbers marked (edit me) and press Run. The equations are plain Python: sin cos atan2 acos sqrt radians degrees pi. Helpers: to_link_angles(s0,s1,s2,s3) servo degrees to link angles, to_servo(yaw,a1,bend2,bend3) link angles to servo degrees. Arm: move(a0,a1,a2,a3) move_xyz(x,y,z,pitch) home() pose() wait(s). Built-in answers to compare with: fk(a0,a1,a2,a3) gives (x,y,z,pitch), ik(x,y,z,pitch) gives the angles or None. No import.
